Start with the hazard

A few actions that matter.

General guidance, not a live assessment. Local authorities’ current instructions take precedence. Source links need internet.

01

Flooding

  1. Avoid floodwater on foot and in vehicles. Turn back from flooded roads and obey barriers.
  2. Move away from low-lying areas toward higher ground before water cuts off access. Follow evacuation instructions.
  3. Stay away from submerged electrical equipment and fallen power lines.

Source: NWS flood guidance ↗

02

Thunder & lightning

  1. When thunder is audible, enter a substantial building or an enclosed, metal-roofed vehicle with its windows closed.
  2. Inside, avoid plumbing, corded equipment, windows, and doors. An isolated tree is not shelter.
  3. Remain sheltered for at least 30 minutes after the last thunder.

Source: NWS lightning guidance ↗

03

Earthquake shaking

  1. Drop, cover, and hold on. Protect your head and neck; use a sturdy table for cover if one is close. If you cannot get down, remain seated and protect your head and neck.
  2. If indoors, stay inside during shaking and away from glass. Do not run outside or use lifts.
  3. If already outdoors, stay outside in an open area away from buildings, poles, and wires.

Source: CDC earthquake guidance ↗

04

Wildfire nearby

  1. Leave promptly when officials order evacuation. If you feel unsafe, do not wait for an order before leaving.
  2. Use current official evacuation directions. Bring your prepared bag and household members if doing so does not delay departure.
  3. Do not return until authorities say you may. A saved map or old message cannot confirm an open route.

Source: U.S. Fire Administration evacuation guidance ↗

05

Cyclone & severe wind

  1. Follow evacuation orders immediately, especially in areas threatened by storm surge or flooding.
  2. If officials advise staying, shelter inside away from windows and glass. Use their designated shelter arrangements.
  3. A brief lull can be the eye of a storm. Do not assume it is over; keep listening for official updates.

Source: NWS tropical-storm guidance ↗

06

Little signal or battery

  1. Keep this downloaded file and important numbers available. A battery-powered radio offers another information channel.
  2. For non-urgent check-ins, try a brief text to your trusted contact. Delivery is not guaranteed; this page does not send messages.
  3. Reduce screen brightness and unnecessary streaming. Keep official emergency alerts enabled on your phone.

Sources: Ready NC communication guidance ↗ · NWS wireless alerts ↗
